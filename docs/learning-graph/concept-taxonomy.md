# Concept Taxonomy

The 358 concepts are grouped into 15 categories. Each category has a TaxonomyID used in `learning-graph.csv`.

## Electrical Fundamentals

**TaxonomyID:** FUND

Electricity, AC power, power factor and three-phase basics that every later concept builds on.

## Power System and Grid Structure

**TaxonomyID:** GRID

The four stages of the power system, interconnections, balancing and generation dispatch.

## Generators and Prime Movers

**TaxonomyID:** GEN

Electromagnetic induction, synchronous generators, ratings, synchronization, and turbines as prime movers.

## Thermal and Nuclear Generation

**TaxonomyID:** THERM

Steam and gas cycles, fossil fuel plants, emissions, and nuclear power.

## Hydro and Renewable Generation

**TaxonomyID:** REN

Hydroelectric, wind, solar PV and other renewable generation, and generation mix cost.

## Energy Storage

**TaxonomyID:** STOR

Batteries, pumped hydro and other storage, plus storage applications.

## Transformers

**TaxonomyID:** XFMR

Transformer principles, ratings, losses, types, connections and instrument transformers.

## Substations

**TaxonomyID:** SUB

Substation types, equipment, buses, grounding and control.

## Transmission Systems

**TaxonomyID:** TRANS

High-voltage lines, conductors, losses, voltage regulation, power flow and grid stability.

## Distribution Systems

**TaxonomyID:** DIST

Feeders, configurations, service drops, automation, smart grid and power factor correction.

## Load Analysis and Utility Rates

**TaxonomyID:** LOAD

Load types, demand and diversity factors, load profiles and utility rate structures.

## Protection and Reliability

**TaxonomyID:** PROT

Faults, protective devices, coordination, arc flash, grounding, and reliability metrics.

## Service Entrance and Backup Power

**TaxonomyID:** SERV

Metering, switchgear, panelboards, service sizing, and standby and emergency power.

## Distributed Energy and Microgrids

**TaxonomyID:** DER

Distributed generation, interconnection, net metering, microgrids and virtual power plants.

## Codes, Economics and Design

**TaxonomyID:** REG

Standards, regulation, efficiency, environmental impact, economics and the electrical design process.
