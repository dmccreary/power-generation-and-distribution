# Hydroelectric and Wind Generation

## Summary

This chapter introduces renewable generation through hydroelectric and wind technologies. Students examine hydro head and flow, dams, reservoir and run-of-river plants, wind resources, wind farms, the Betz limit, capacity factor, and offshore installations. They will be able to estimate the factors that control energy production from water and wind.
