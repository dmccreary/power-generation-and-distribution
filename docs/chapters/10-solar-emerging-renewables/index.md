# Solar and Emerging Renewable Technologies

## Summary

This chapter develops photovoltaic generation from solar irradiance and individual cells through modules, arrays, strings, ratings, orientation, inverters, and maximum power point tracking. It compares rooftop, utility-scale, and concentrated solar installations before surveying geothermal, biomass, biogas, waste-to-energy, marine, and hydrogen technologies. Students will be able to identify suitable renewable technologies and the variables governing their output.
