# Transformers and Instrument-Based Protection

## Summary

This chapter develops transformer operating principles and the measurements that support protection. Students analyze turns ratio, ratings, impedance, losses, efficiency, cooling, construction types, polarity, banks, autotransformers, current and potential transformers, fault current, relays, and differential protection. They will be able to relate transformer characteristics to equipment selection and protective-system behavior.
