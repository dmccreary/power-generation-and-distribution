# Efficiency, Sustainability, and Design Synthesis

## Summary

This chapter synthesizes technical, economic, and environmental reasoning into the electrical design process. Students connect efficiency, conservation, electrification, decarbonization, emissions, life-cycle impacts, audits, load calculations, energy plans, single-line diagrams, and equipment specifications. They will complete the progression by organizing evidence and design decisions into a coherent power-system report.
