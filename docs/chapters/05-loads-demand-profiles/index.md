# Loads, Demand, and Facility Profiles

## Summary

This chapter examines how facilities use electrical power over time. Students classify major load types and analyze connected load, demand load, peak demand, demand factor, diversity, coincidence, load factor, forecasting, and load profiles. They will be able to interpret facility load behavior as preparation for rates, equipment sizing, storage, and service design.
