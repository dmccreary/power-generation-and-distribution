# Thermal and Nuclear Generation

## Summary

This chapter surveys the equipment and thermodynamic processes used in thermal and nuclear power plants. Students compare Rankine and Brayton cycles, fossil-fuel technologies, heat rate, emissions controls, carbon capture, reactor types, fuel cycles, and nuclear safety. They will be able to evaluate how plant design affects efficiency, emissions, and grid performance.
