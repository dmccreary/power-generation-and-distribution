# Faults, Grounding, and Overcurrent Fundamentals

## Summary

This chapter establishes the physical and safety foundations of power-system protection. Students distinguish overloads, short circuits, ground faults, line-to-line faults, and three-phase faults while examining grounding, fuses, overcurrent protection, ground-fault protection, and surge protection. They will be prepared to study fault-current calculations and coordinated protective equipment.
