# Energy Storage and Generation Evaluation

## Summary

This chapter combines economic evaluation of generation with the operating roles of energy storage. Students compare generation mixes and levelized cost while examining batteries, pumped hydro, flywheels, thermal storage, compressed air, state of charge, conversion systems, round-trip efficiency, peak shaving, and arbitrage. They will be able to match storage technologies and operating strategies to grid and facility needs.
