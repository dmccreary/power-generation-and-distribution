# Building Service, Metering, Automation, and Backup Power

## Summary

This chapter carries electrical power from the utility connection into a building and its critical loads. Students examine metering, service sizing and voltage selection, disconnects, switchgear, switchboards, panels, motor control, distribution, smart-grid automation, demand response, standby and emergency systems, generators, transfer switches, and UPS equipment. They will be able to organize a reliable building service architecture.
