# Generators and Prime Movers

## Summary

This chapter explains how mechanical energy becomes electrical energy. It develops electromagnetic induction, generator construction, excitation, ratings, speed, synchronization, prime movers, turbine types, cogeneration, combined-cycle plants, and combined heat and power. Students will be able to relate generator characteristics to the mechanical systems that drive them.
