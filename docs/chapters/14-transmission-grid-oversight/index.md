# Transmission Systems and Grid Oversight

## Summary

This chapter follows bulk electrical power across high-voltage transmission systems. Students examine conductors, towers, insulation, rights-of-way, underground and HVDC systems, losses, voltage regulation, line parameters, power flow, congestion, stability, transformer taps, and federal oversight. They will be able to explain how transmission design and operation move power reliably over long distances.
