# Standards, Distributed Energy, and Microgrids

## Summary

This chapter connects distributed energy design to the standards governing safe installation and interconnection. Students apply NEC, NESC, IEEE, ANSI, and NFPA frameworks to distributed generation, net metering, anti-islanding, behind-the-meter systems, solar-plus-storage, microgrids, islanding, and virtual power plants. They will be able to outline an interconnection pathway and identify the governing requirements.
