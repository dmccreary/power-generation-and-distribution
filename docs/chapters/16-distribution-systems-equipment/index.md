# Distribution Systems and Equipment

## Summary

This chapter traces power from the distribution substation toward customer premises. Students compare radial, loop, network, overhead, and underground systems while examining voltage levels, feeders, reclosers, cutouts, sectionalizers, voltage drop, service connections, and pole- and pad-mounted transformers. They will be able to compare distribution configurations in terms of operation, reliability, and application.
