# Substations and Protection Coordination

## Summary

This chapter integrates transformation, switching, control, and protection within substations. Students study substation types, bus arrangements, breakers, disconnects, arresters, regulators, grounding, control houses, subtransmission, interrupting ratings, time-current curves, coordination, and arc-flash hazards. They will be able to trace equipment functions and evaluate the sequence of protective operation.
