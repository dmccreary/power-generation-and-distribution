# Chapters

This textbook is organized into 20 chapters covering 358 concepts.

## Chapter Overview

1. [Electrical Foundations](01-electrical-foundations/index.md) - Introduces electric charge, voltage, current, resistance, energy, DC and AC, magnetic fields, waveforms, frequency, and the foundational quantities used throughout the power system.
2. [AC Circuits and Power Factor](02-ac-circuits-power-factor/index.md) - Develops impedance, reactance, inductance, capacitance, load behavior, real and reactive power, apparent power, the power triangle, and leading and lagging power factor.
3. [Three-Phase Systems and Power-System Representation](03-three-phase-system-representation/index.md) - Explains single- and three-phase power, balanced and unbalanced loads, wye and delta connections, voltage relationships, power-system stages, per-unit quantities, and single-line diagrams.
4. [Grid Architecture and System Operations](04-grid-architecture-operations/index.md) - Traces the interconnected electric grid through balancing authorities and regional organizations while examining reliability, frequency control, supply-demand balance, generation dispatch, reserves, and load following.
5. [Loads, Demand, and Facility Profiles](05-loads-demand-profiles/index.md) - Classifies residential, commercial, industrial, motor, lighting, vehicle-charging, and data-center loads while developing demand, diversity, coincidence, load-factor, forecasting, and load-profile analysis.
6. [Utility Rates and Project Economics](06-utility-rates-project-economics/index.md) - Connects utility regulation and rate structures with energy, demand, time-of-use, and power-factor charges, then introduces capital cost, operating cost, incentives, payback, return, and present-value analysis.
7. [Generators and Prime Movers](07-generators-prime-movers/index.md) - Develops electromagnetic induction, generator construction and operation, excitation, ratings, speed, synchronization, prime movers, turbines, cogeneration, combined-cycle generation, and combined heat and power.
8. [Thermal and Nuclear Generation](08-thermal-nuclear-generation/index.md) - Examines thermal cycles, boilers, condensers, cooling, efficiency, heat rate, fossil-fuel plants, emissions controls, carbon capture, nuclear reactors, fuel cycles, safety, and small modular reactors.
9. [Hydroelectric and Wind Generation](09-hydroelectric-wind-generation/index.md) - Introduces renewable energy through hydroelectric and wind systems, including dams, head and flow, reservoir and run-of-river plants, wind resources, turbine limits, capacity factor, and offshore wind.
10. [Solar and Emerging Renewable Technologies](10-solar-emerging-renewables/index.md) - Builds from solar irradiance and photovoltaic cells through arrays, ratings, string configurations, inverters, tracking, rooftop and utility installations, then surveys geothermal, biomass, marine, and hydrogen technologies.
11. [Energy Storage and Generation Evaluation](11-energy-storage-generation-evaluation/index.md) - Compares generation mixes and levelized cost while examining battery, pumped-hydro, flywheel, thermal, and compressed-air storage, including state of charge, conversion systems, efficiency, peak shaving, and arbitrage.
12. [Faults, Grounding, and Overcurrent Fundamentals](12-faults-grounding-overcurrent/index.md) - Establishes fault and short-circuit behavior, fault types, grounding, overloads, fuses, overcurrent and ground-fault protection, and surge protection before more advanced equipment and coordination studies.
13. [Transformers and Instrument-Based Protection](13-transformers-instrument-protection/index.md) - Covers transformer principles, ratios, ratings, impedance, losses, cooling, construction, polarity, banks, autotransformers, instrument transformers, available fault current, protective relays, and differential protection.
14. [Transmission Systems and Grid Oversight](14-transmission-grid-oversight/index.md) - Examines overhead, underground, and HVDC transmission, conductors, towers, insulation, losses, voltage regulation, line parameters, power flow, congestion, stability, transformer taps, and federal reliability oversight.
15. [Substations and Protection Coordination](15-substations-protection-coordination/index.md) - Integrates substation types, buses, breakers, switches, arresters, regulators, grounding, control houses, subtransmission, interrupting ratings, time-current curves, protection coordination, and arc-flash hazards.
16. [Distribution Systems and Equipment](16-distribution-systems-equipment/index.md) - Traces primary and secondary distribution through radial, loop, network, overhead, and underground systems while covering feeders, service connections, sectionalizing equipment, voltage drop, and distribution transformers.
17. [Distribution Performance and Reliability](17-distribution-reliability/index.md) - Develops distribution automation, Volt-VAR control, power-factor correction, capacitor sizing, reliability indices, redundancy, outage analysis, single points of failure, and system resiliency.
18. [Building Service, Metering, Automation, and Backup Power](18-building-service-metering-backup/index.md) - Follows power through metering, service equipment, switchgear, panels, motor control, and building distribution while connecting smart-grid automation, demand response, standby systems, generators, transfer switches, and UPS equipment.
19. [Standards, Distributed Energy, and Microgrids](19-standards-der-microgrids/index.md) - Applies NEC, NESC, IEEE, ANSI, and NFPA requirements to distributed resources, interconnection, net metering, anti-islanding, behind-the-meter generation, solar-plus-storage, microgrids, islanding, and virtual power plants.
20. [Efficiency, Sustainability, and Design Synthesis](20-efficiency-sustainable-design/index.md) - Integrates efficiency, conservation, electrification, decarbonization, emissions, environmental assessment, load calculations, energy planning, single-line design, equipment specifications, and the culminating power-system report.

## How to Use This Textbook

Begin with the electrical foundations and proceed in chapter order, because each chapter is positioned after the concepts it depends on. Use the concept tables and prerequisite links to review earlier material before advancing into protection, distribution, distributed energy, and design synthesis.

**Note:** Each chapter includes a list of concepts covered. Make sure to complete prerequisites before moving to advanced chapters.
