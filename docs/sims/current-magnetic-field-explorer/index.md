## About This MicroSim

This activity combines the right-hand grip rule with the field-magnitude equation for an ideal long straight conductor. Four prediction cases isolate current reversal, current doubling, and distance doubling before exploration controls are unlocked.

## How to Use

Choose the predicted circulation direction and enter the field magnitude in microteslas. Direction has one scored attempt and magnitude has two. In exploration mode, change one quantity and predict whether the field magnitude increased, decreased, or stayed the same.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

Learners will demonstrate how current direction, current magnitude, and distance determine the direction and magnitude of the magnetic field around a long straight conductor.

### Prerequisites

- Conventional current and magnetic fields
- Vector direction and the right-hand grip rule
- Tesla and microtesla
- Into-the-page and out-of-the-page notation

### Suggested Activity

1. Require a hand-rule prediction before learners enter each numerical result.
2. Compare cases 1 and 2 to isolate direction reversal.
3. Compare cases 1, 3, and 4 to describe proportional and inverse relationships.

### Assessment

Mastery is four correct directions and at least three of four magnitudes on the first attempt. The exploration comparison provides immediate formative feedback.
