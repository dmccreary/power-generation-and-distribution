# Character Sheet: Relay the Beaver

The canonical identity document for Relay, the pedagogical mascot for the
**Power Generation and Distribution** textbook. Every pose prompt and every
piece of AI-generated content involving Relay must re-anchor to the description
below.

## Identity

- **Name:** Relay
- **Species:** North American beaver
- **Subject:** Electric power generation, transmission, and distribution
- **Catchphrase:** “Let’s follow the flow!”

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Warm chestnut brown — hex `#8D5A3B`
- **Accent colors:** Cream muzzle and inner ears — hex `#F3D8B6`; indigo hard hat — hex `#3F51B5`; safety orange vest — hex `#FB8C00`
- **Clothing / accessories:** Indigo hard hat with a small orange lightning-bolt emblem, orange high-visibility safety vest with silver reflective stripes, compact brown tool belt, and a dark indigo tablet displaying a simple unlabeled single-line electrical diagram
- **Expression:** Bright, kind eyes, prominent beaver incisors, and a confident closed-mouth smile
- **Size proportion:** Compact, rounded, icon-friendly silhouette with a broad flat tail
- **Art style:** Polished modern flat-vector educational illustration with clean dark outlines and subtle texture; professional enough for college and adult trades learners

## Personality

- Practical
- Curious
- Patient
- Safety-minded

## Voice

- Explains systems by tracing the path of energy from source to load
- Encouraging and concise, with occasional restrained electrical or beaver puns
- Treats learners as capable future electrical designers
- Signature phrases: “Let’s follow the flow!”, “Check the connection.”, “Build it safe and reliable.”

## Why This Mascot

Beavers are practical system builders, making Relay a natural guide for a book
about the interconnected electric grid. The name “Relay” refers to a protective
power-system device while also suggesting the staged movement of electrical
energy from generation to the customer.
