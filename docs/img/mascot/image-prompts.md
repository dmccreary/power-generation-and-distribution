# Relay Mascot Image Prompts

Generate seven different poses for one book mascot. Maintain exactly the same
character design, proportions, colors, clothing, line work, and rendering style
across all seven images.

## Neutral

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent educational mascot cutout.
Create Relay, a friendly anthropomorphic North American beaver mascot for a
college-level Power Generation and Distribution textbook. Relay has warm
chestnut-brown fur (`#8D5A3B`), a cream muzzle and inner ears (`#F3D8B6`),
bright kind eyes, prominent beaver incisors, small rounded ears, sturdy compact
limbs, and a broad flat crosshatched tail. Relay wears an indigo hard hat
(`#3F51B5`) with a small orange lightning-bolt emblem, an orange high-visibility
safety vest (`#FB8C00`) with silver reflective stripes, and a compact brown tool
belt. Polished modern flat-vector educational illustration, clean dark outlines,
subtle texture, professional rather than childish, compact icon-friendly
silhouette. Relay stands upright facing the viewer in a relaxed neutral pose,
arms resting naturally at their sides, with a confident closed-mouth smile.
Centered full-body composition with all extremities and tail visible. Genuine
RGBA transparent background with alpha channel and generous clear separation
from the canvas edges. No words, letters, labels, watermark, scenery, border,
white background, black background, or checkerboard background.
