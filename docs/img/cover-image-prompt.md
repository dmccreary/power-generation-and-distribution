# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook. This
image will be used on the textbook home page and in social media previews.

**Required specifications:**

- Format: PNG
- Wide-landscape format
- Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
- This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, unified color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

## Subject & Tone

**Power Generation and Distribution** is a college-level textbook that follows
electrical energy from power plants and renewable sources through generators,
transformers, high-voltage transmission, substations, distribution feeders,
and the service entrance of a commercial or industrial building. It also
covers three-phase AC power, protection, energy storage, microgrids, and the
design decisions required for safe, reliable, efficient electrical systems.

The intended audience is undergraduate electrical design, estimating, and
construction-management students, along with adult electrical-trades learners
moving into design roles. The visual tone should be modern, technical,
energetic, precise, and approachable for capable future electrical designers.

## Title

Render this exact title, verbatim, and no other words:

**POWER GENERATION AND DISTRIBUTION**

Place the title at the center in a bold, clean, highly legible geometric
sans-serif typeface. Use white or very light cream lettering with a subtle dark
shadow or translucent navy scrim. Break the title into two balanced lines if
needed, but do not abbreviate it. Preserve generous negative space directly
behind the title. Do not allow wires, towers, diagrams, or other montage
elements to cross through the letters.

## Style & Composition

- Illustration style: polished isometric technical illustration blended with
  modern flat-vector educational art; crisp silhouettes, clean dark outlines,
  subtle dimensional shading, and consistent rendering across every element.
- Color palette: deep indigo and midnight navy, electric teal and cyan, with
  controlled safety-orange and warm amber highlights.
- Lighting and mood: bright, optimistic, and energized, with restrained glowing
  power-flow accents against a rich dark-blue background.
- Composition: left-to-right visual journey from generation to end use;
  centered title; montage elements forming a loose panoramic ring around it;
  Relay in the lower-left; generous title-safe negative space.
- Hierarchy: title first, source-to-load power path second, mascot third,
  supporting technical motifs last.

## Constraints

- Render the title exactly as **POWER GENERATION AND DISTRIBUTION**.
- Include no subtitle, author name, labels, captions, numbers, logos, UI

- Keep all essential content inside a safe margin of at least 60 pixels.
